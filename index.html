    Good to know

    What do the statuses mean?
      What counts as urgent?

      Tick urgent only if the problem is dangerous or cannot wait: flooding, fallen wires, a blocked road in an emergency, or someone in danger. Urgent reports are shown to the officials first.

      How do I rate the officials?

      When a report is Resolved you can give 1 to 5 stars (5 is the best) and a comment. One rating per report.

      Why can't I see my report on another phone or computer?

      Reports are saved in the browser you used. Use Track My Report and type the report number (for example BRG-0007) to bring it into this browser.
